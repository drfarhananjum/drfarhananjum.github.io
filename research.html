Research interests

01

Cancer, metabolism and cellular adaptation

How metabolic reprogramming and cellular stress reshape chromatin architecture, mitochondrial function, lysosomal dynamics and inter-organelle communication in cancer, metabolic and age-associated disease, and how these nanoscale changes steer cell-state transitions and disease progression.

02

Chromatin architecture, DNA-RNA interaction and genome regulation

How euchromatin and heterochromatin organisation, chromatin compaction, 3D genome architecture and epigenetic states are remodelled during disease, and how this affects transcription and genome stability.

03

Causal and perturbation-based cell biology

CRISPR/Cas genetic perturbation, fluorescent knock-in and knockout, and live-cell imaging, to move from observing phenotypes to understanding mechanism.

04

Super-resolution and single-molecule imaging

STORM, SRRF and SRRF-Stream, single-molecule imaging and tracking, smFISH and quantitative live-cell fluorescence microscopy.

05

Genome and epigenome profiling

Hi-C, ChIP-seq and CUT&Tag, ATAC-seq, and transcriptomic and single-cell approaches, to connect imaging phenotypes with genome organisation and gene regulation.

06

Quantitative and multimodal imaging

Joining super-resolution imaging, quantitative image analysis, spatial and genomic assays and computational methods to link molecular organisation, cellular dynamics and function.

07

Translational direction

Quantitative imaging and molecular signatures that could serve as biomarkers of disease progression, cell state and therapeutic response.
